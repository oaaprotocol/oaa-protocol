## Fees after launch

- Launchpad tokens: 1% per trade → 0.4% creator, 0.3% $OAA buyback & burn, 0.3% treasury.
- Agentverse swaps: optional platform fee set by `SWAP_FEE_BPS` (default 0.3%).
- Buy $OAA with any coin: 0.3% app fee to the treasury (`BUY_FEE_BPS`).
- x402 services: $0.05 to $5 per call, paid in USDC to the treasury.
