Launchpad setup

This page deploys the OAA launchpad contract on Base from your own wallet and tests it for free before anyone uses it. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the Base network through your wallet.

1Wallet

Use the wallet that should own the launchpad. You need a little ETH on Base for the deploy (usually well below $5).

2Settings

Only the treasury is yours to fill in. It receives the 1% trading fees of every token. A Safe multisig is recommended.

Contract addresses (already filled in, checked in step 3)

3Check

Reads every address from Base and checks that it is really what it should be. Free, no transaction.

    4Deploy

    Your wallet asks you to confirm one transaction. After that the launchpad exists on Base.

    5Free test on Base

    Simulates a token launch on the real Aerodrome contracts, once without and once with a small first buy. It is only a simulation: nothing is created and it costs nothing.

      6Next

      When step 5 is all green, send the launchpad address to your developer so the website can be switched live. Optional, but recommended:

      • Verify on BaseScan (so everyone can read the code): BaseScan → Verify & Publish → Solidity (Standard-Json-Input), compiler v0.7.6+commit.7338295f, file standard-json-input.json, contract oaa/OAALaunchpad.sol:OAALaunchpad, constructor arguments from step 4.
      • Hand ownership to the Safe: the owner can only pause launches and change the treasury. The button sends the ownership to the treasury; the Safe then confirms it with acceptOwnership().

      7Buyback (owner only)

      0.3% of every trade is saved in the launchpad for buybacks. Once $OAA has a pool on Aerodrome Slipstream, set it here once. After that the owner (or a keeper you choose) buys $OAA with the saved ETH; the contract always sends the tokens to the burn address.

      The launchpad contract has not been audited by an independent firm. Deploying it is your decision. Liquidity added by launches is locked forever and cannot be withdrawn by anyone, including you.
